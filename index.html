Morgan Byers

About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Colorado - Boulder, advised by Liz Bradley. My research is focused on the spatial analysis of topological signatures in complex dynamical systems.

When I'm not working on research, you can usually find me reading a good book, trying out a new baking recipe, or hanging upside down from a lyra, silks, or other aerial apparatus.
